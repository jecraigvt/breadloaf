A little guidance

Familiar by design.
Different in feeling.

Every direction keeps Hub / Dates / Rooms / Guide / Board, the same destinations, and the same everyday tasks. The choice is how you want the place to feel.

Our recommendation

Start with Refined Cabin.

It gives returning family members the most recognizable experience, with enough polish to clearly feel new. Choose Family Field Guide for more atmosphere, or Modern Homestead for a more compact everyday workspace.

You can also choose one direction and borrow a detail you love from another.

Design demonstration only. All visits, documents, and conversations are sample content. Nothing here books a room, uploads a file, contacts Bucky, or changes the live site.
